# 1. Purpose

The previous AI Platform documents define how PesoPilot is designed and implemented today.

This document defines how the platform can evolve over the next five to ten years without requiring fundamental architectural redesign.

Rather than introducing new implementation details, this document establishes the long-term architectural direction of the AI Platform.

The goal is to ensure that future capabilities—including multiple language models, specialized AI agents, tool ecosystems, enterprise governance, and autonomous routing—can be introduced incrementally while preserving the architecture established in Documents **12.0–12.9**.

---

# 2. AI Evolution Philosophy

PesoPilot follows one long-term architectural principle.

> **Workflows request AI capabilities—not specific models.**

Business workflows should never know:

* provider names,
* model names,
* API versions,
* deployment locations,
* infrastructure details.

Instead, workflows describe the capability they require.

The platform determines how that capability is fulfilled.

# 5. Model Router

The Model Router is introduced as a future architectural subsystem.

Its responsibility is determining which model should execute a workflow.

Unlike the Provider Layer, which knows how to communicate with providers, the Model Router decides which provider and model best satisfy a requested capability.

Responsibilities include:

* capability resolution,
* provider selection,
* model selection,
* fallback routing,
* latency optimization,
* cost optimization,
* health-aware routing,
* policy-aware routing.

The Model Router does not execute models.

# 10. Cost Optimization

Examples:

* Simple classifications may execute on lightweight local models.
* Financial summaries may execute on larger reasoning models.
* Vision workflows may execute on specialized multimodal models.

This approach balances quality, latency, and operational cost.

# 16. Architecture Decision Records

## ADR-356

**Decision**

Workflows request capabilities instead of model names.

**Reason**

Reduces coupling and enables provider-independent evolution.

---

## ADR-357

**Decision**

Introduce a dedicated Model Router.

**Reason**

Separates provider communication from model selection.

---

## ADR-358

**Decision**

Introduce AI Capability Profiles.

**Reason**

Allows routing decisions to be based on functionality rather than implementation.

---

## ADR-359

**Decision**

Maintain a centralized AI Registry.

**Reason**

Simplifies provider onboarding, governance, and operational management.

---

## ADR-360

**Decision**

Design the platform for incremental evolution.

**Reason**

Allows future AI capabilities to be introduced without changing the established architecture.
